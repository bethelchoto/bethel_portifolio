Vertex Legal AI

Overview

Vertex Legal AI is a pioneering legaltech platform designed for Zimbabwe, delivering accessible, AI-powered legal services directly to individuals and businesses. The platform bridges the gap between Zimbabweans and quality legal guidance — providing instant AI-driven legal advice, intelligent document analysis, and seamless lawyer matching in a single, user-friendly interface. I designed and developed the website to communicate the brand's innovative vision while establishing trust and authority in the legal space.

Key Features

AI-Powered Legal Guidance

An intelligent chat interface allowing users to ask legal questions and receive instant, accurate guidance powered by advanced AI — making legal knowledge accessible to everyone, regardless of their ability to afford traditional legal consultations.

Document Analysis

Smart document scanning and analysis tools that review contracts, agreements, and legal papers — identifying risks, flagging problematic clauses, and providing plain-English summaries to help users understand complex legal documents before signing.

Lawyer Matching System

An intelligent matchmaking feature connecting users with qualified Zimbabwean attorneys based on their specific legal matter, location, and budget — reducing the friction of finding trustworthy legal representation.

Trust & Credibility Design

Carefully crafted UI/UX with professional colour palette, clear calls-to-action, and trust-building elements (testimonials, lawyer profiles, compliance badges) to position Vertex Legal AI as a credible and reliable platform in a high-stakes industry.

Fully Responsive Design

Optimised for all screen sizes — mobile, tablet, and desktop — ensuring the platform is accessible to Zimbabweans on any device, including mobile-first users.

Technology Stack

Next.js React Tailwind CSS TypeScript Framer Motion OpenAI API Vercel

  • Frontend → Next.js & React Built with Next.js for server-side rendering, fast page loads, and optimal SEO — ensuring the platform ranks highly for legal service searches and loads quickly across Zimbabwe's varied network conditions.
  • Styling → Tailwind CSS Tailwind CSS enabled the rapid construction of a clean, professional design system aligned with the Vertex Legal AI brand — conveying authority, trust, and innovation.
  • Language → TypeScript TypeScript was used throughout the codebase to ensure type safety, reduce runtime errors, and improve developer experience as the platform continues to scale.
  • Animations → Framer Motion Subtle scroll-triggered animations and smooth page transitions enhance the user experience without distracting from the platform's core functionality.
  • Deployment → Vercel Hosted on Vercel for automatic HTTPS, global CDN distribution, and zero-downtime deployments — ensuring the platform is always available and fast for users across Zimbabwe and beyond.

Challenges & Solutions

  • Building Trust in a Sensitive Domain Legal services require high levels of user trust. Addressed this through meticulous attention to UI/UX, professional copywriting, transparent privacy policies, and clear disclaimers that distinguish AI guidance from formal legal advice.
  • Accessibility & Inclusion With Zimbabwe's diverse user base and varied internet quality, the site was optimised for performance using Next.js image optimisation, lazy loading, and minimal third-party dependencies to ensure a smooth experience on slower connections.
  • Local Legal Context Ensuring the platform's content and features accurately reflect Zimbabwean legal frameworks, statutes, and processes — making it genuinely useful rather than generic.
