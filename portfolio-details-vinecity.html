🏘️ VineCity Housing Trust Backend

A production-ready, highly modular monolith backend for the VineCity Housing Trust — managing land allocation workflows, installment-based payment schedules, granular role-based permissions, and director-level financial reports.

Built with TypeScript 5/6, Node.js, Express 5, raw MariaDB connection pool (mysql2/promise), Multer for bulk file parsing, Nodemailer & Resend for secure transactional email dispatching, and a background installment scheduler. Deployed and orchestrated via Docker Compose on a Linux VPS with Nginx reverse proxy and SSL.

Domain Model & Features Overview

The platform is designed around strict database integrity rules, transaction auditing, and a highly granular permissions system:

👤 1. Authentication & Password-Less Invites
  • Stateless JWT Authorization: Secures sessions with cryptographically signed tokens containing role and module claims.
  • Two-Step Email Activation: Registers standard profiles with SMTP-dispatched 6-digit OTP verification codes.
  • Staff & Director Invite Workflows: Admin issues a secure invite_token that allows directors/staff to set their password through a personalized onboarding link without pre-registering.
🏘️ 2. Development Phases & Stands Catalog
  • Multi-Phase Infrastructure: Phases act as target housing development projects, allowing administrators to enable or disable phases and all child stands.
  • Rich Land Use Attributes: Tracks detailed stand profiles with land usage designations, sizes, base valuations, and availability statuses.
  • Bulk Spreadsheet Importing: Allows staff to upload .xlsx or .csv files via Multer to import hundreds of stands at once.
📅 3. Land Allocations
  • Conflict Prevention Lock: Implements strict application logic checking for existing ACTIVE allocations, preventing a stand from being dual-allocated.
  • JSON Metadata Assets: Stores up to 4 Next of Kin and 4 Beneficiary profiles directly inside the allocation ledger using JSON fields.
  • Auditable Lifecycle: Tracks allocation status (ACTIVE, DEALLOCATED), deallocation timestamps, and the recording operator.
💰 4. Payment Ledger & Installments
  • Unified Payment Contract: Automatically generates payment records representing the total contracted stand value, amount paid, and outstanding balance.
  • Installment Generation Engine: Automatically breaks down payment contracts into configurable installments with customized due dates and amounts.
  • Diverse Transaction Modes: Supports multiple payment types (ONCE_OFF, INSTALLMENT, DONATION, STAFF, REPLACE) and methods (CASH, BANK, CARD, ECOCASH).
  • Debt Write-offs: Supports write-off adjustments (amount_write_off) to balance accounts in dispute or for special allocations.
💸 5. Expense Tracking
  • Operational & Daily Bookkeeping: Tracks company expenditures categorised by type (OPERATIONAL, DAILY), detailing descriptions, amounts, and dates.
  • Physical Signatures & Collections: Logs who authorized the expense, who collected the cash, and saves signature data for administrative accountability.
🛡️ 6. Granular RBAC Permissions
  • Granular Module Rules: Restricts access on a module-action basis (users, stands, phases, allocations, payments, installments, expenses, reports paired with read, write, manage, delete).
  • User Overrides: Admins can define broad role permissions or apply custom overrides for specific individual users.
📊 7. Director Financial Dashboards
  • High-Level Financial Summaries: Financial collections aggregated into contracted value, paid volume, outstanding debt, and collection rates.
  • Risk Metrics & Aging Reports: Group outstanding debts into 30, 60, 90, and 90+ day buckets.
  • Defaulter Registers: Identifies stands with low payment percentages compared to expectations.
